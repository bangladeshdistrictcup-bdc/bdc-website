Privacy Policy

How we collect and use your information

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register a team or contact us, we may collect: team name, player/representative name, mobile number, WhatsApp number, address, district, and other details you submit through our forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for tournament management — registration confirmation, match updates, communication with teams, and related organizational purposes.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing. Information may be shared only with organizers and officials as needed to run the tournament.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: bangladeshdistrictcup@gmail.com or via our Contact page.
