Rules for participating in Bangladesh District Cup

Last updated: August 2026

1. Acceptance

By registering a team or using this website, you agree to these Terms & Conditions and all official tournament rules published by the BDC organizing committee.

2. Eligibility

Teams must represent a district or local area of Bangladesh. All players must meet age and eligibility criteria set by the organizers.

3. Registration

All information submitted during registration must be accurate. False information may result in disqualification. Registration is subject to approval by the organizing committee.

4. Conduct

Players, officials and supporters must maintain sportsmanship. Violence, abuse, or serious misconduct may lead to bans or legal action.

5. Decisions

All decisions of the BDC organizing committee regarding matches, discipline, protests and tournament matters shall be final.

6. Changes

The organizers reserve the right to update these terms, tournament format, schedule or rules as needed. Updates will be posted on this website and social media.

7. Contact

For questions about these terms, contact: bangladeshdistrictcup@gmail.com
