Rules & Regulations

Official guidelines for Bangladesh District Cup (will be updated)

Player Rules

  • Players must be of eligible age as defined by the committee.
  • Each player can represent only one team.
  • Valid identification may be required.

Match Duration

  • Standard match duration will be announced.
  • Extra time and penalties apply in knockout stages.

Card Rules

  • Yellow card = Warning. Two yellows = Red card.
  • Red card = Dismissal + possible suspension.

Substitution Rules

  • Number of substitutions will follow announced rules.
  • Substitutes must be listed before kick-off.

Discipline Rules

  • Violent conduct or abusive language will result in strong sanctions.
  • Team officials are also subject to disciplinary action.

⚖️ Protest Rules

  • Protests must be submitted in writing within the stipulated time.
  • The decision of the organizing committee shall be final.

* Full official rulebook will be published before the tournament starts.
