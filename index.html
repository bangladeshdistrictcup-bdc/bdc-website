About the Tournament

More Than Just a Football Cup

Bangladesh District Cup (BDC) is a nationwide initiative dedicated to uniting passionate football communities from districts and local regions across Bangladesh. We believe football is more than a game — it promotes discipline, leadership, teamwork and national unity.

Our goal is to discover emerging talent, inspire the next generation, and create a transparent competitive platform where every team has an equal opportunity to shine.

Our Vision

To become Bangladesh's most respected, transparent, and influential community football competition.

Our Mission

To organize fair, competitive, and professionally managed football tournaments that empower players and strengthen local football communities.
